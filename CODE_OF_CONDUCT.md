# Code of Conduct

Quadrivium welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience,
or viewpoint. Project spaces should support respectful questions, technical
disagreement, and constructive collaboration without harassment.

## Participate constructively

Discuss ideas and code with care for the people contributing them. Explain
technical criticism with evidence, make room for different experience levels,
and respond to questions respectfully. Take responsibility for mistakes and
repair harm when appropriate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
doxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project
publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may
restrict participation temporarily or permanently when necessary.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through the contact options on their
GitHub profile. Avoid posting sensitive details in public issues. Reports are
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. It describes
the project's participation expectations; [Security](SECURITY.md) separately
covers vulnerability reports, and [Support](SUPPORT.md) covers technical help.
